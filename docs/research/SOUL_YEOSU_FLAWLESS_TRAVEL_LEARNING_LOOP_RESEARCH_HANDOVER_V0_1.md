## A. CORE DEFINITION

A regional travel intelligence learning loop that learns:

1. How travel professionals design successful journeys
2. What travelers ask before and during travel
3. Where travelers experience friction
4. Where expectation differs from reality
5. What travelers wish they had known earlier
6. What unexpectedly delighted them
7. How plans actually change
8. What should therefore be prepared for the next traveler

...and feeds validated findings back into Prepared Knowledge / Prepared Context.

---

## B. LEARNING LOOP

```
Travel Agency / YTC
→ Traveler Question
→ Actual Journey
→ Traveler Reaction / Friction / Delight / Regret
→ Founder / Local Operator Validation
→ Phoenix Verification
→ Prepared Knowledge / Prepared Context
→ SOUL Contextual Assistance
→ Next Actual Journey
→ new learning signals
→ loop
```

**Discipline:** Repeated traveler signals are Knowledge Need evidence, not automatically factual Evidence.  
Founder / Local Operator validation is required before feeding signals into Prepared Knowledge.  
Do not treat every traveler reaction as Truth.

## E. FLAWLESS TRAVEL DEFINITION (WORKING)

"Flawless Travel" does NOT mean no unexpected event, delay, weather issue, fatigue, closure, or plan disruption occurs.

It means:
- Foreseeable friction is reduced where possible
- Previously observed traveler mistakes are not unnecessarily repeated
- Important context is remembered
- Necessary information is surfaced at the right moment
- When plans change, the traveler is given a viable next choice
- The journey can continue without unnecessary confusion

**Working product expression (WORKING EXPRESSION — not approved brand copy):**  
"여행할수록 더 똑똑해지고, 다음 여행자의 마음을 더 잘 알아주는 여수여행."

## F. "UNDERSTANDS MY HEART" OPERATIONALIZATION (WORKING)

NOT emotion inference. Operational interpretation:

- Do not re-ask information already supplied
- Anticipate foreseeable friction when evidence supports it
- Narrow choices according to known traveler context
- Ask only minimum necessary missing context
- Adapt when plans change
- Preserve journey continuity
- Surface useful knowledge before avoidable regret occurs
