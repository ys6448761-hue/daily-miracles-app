## 5. Problem with Demographic-Label Judgment

**The label-based pattern:**
- Visitor is "senior/elderly" → difficult / suitable / unsuitable
- Visitor is "child" → suitable / unsuitable

**Problems with this pattern:**
1. **Demographic proxy error:** Age category is a proxy for mobility capability, not capability itself. A 75-year-old experienced hiker and a 60-year-old with knee replacement have very different Hyangiram experiences.
2. **Category over-generalization:** "Senior-friendly" means different things at different places. The label doesn't carry the friction model.
3. **Capability erasure:** The label approach skips the decision-relevant variable: can this specific traveler manage this specific friction?
4. **Minimizing question quality:** "Is it okay for elderly people?" requires a demographic policy answer. "Can they walk steep stairs?" requires only a brief capability clarification — and then SOUL can give a useful, specific answer.

## 6. Four-Layer Distinction

| Layer | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Traveler Preference** | Subjective taste, aesthetic inclination | "I prefer quiet places" / "I like scenic views" |
| **Traveler State** | Current situational context: location, time, companion, vehicle, energy, budget, weather | With parents / in Yeosu now / no car / half-day remaining |
| **Traveler Condition / Capability** | Ability or sensitivity relevant to a specific experience | Stair capability / slope tolerance / motion sensitivity / height anxiety |
| **Experience Requirement / Friction** | What the place/activity physically or practically demands of the traveler | ~400 stairs / steep incline / narrow rock passages / no elevator |

**These four layers are distinct and must not be collapsed:**
- Traveler Preference does not reveal Capability
- Traveler State does not reveal Capability
- Demographic category does not reveal Capability
- Capability must be elicited or known separately

## 7. Conditional Judgment Model (Research Chain)

```
[Traveler State]
  + [Traveler Condition / Capability]
  + [Experience Requirement / Friction — from Prepared Knowledge]
  + [Journey Context]
  + [Live Condition — if VOLATILE field]
  ↓
[Friction Assessment]
  → Friction present?
  → Mitigation/alternative available?
  → Residual burden?
  ↓
[SUFFICIENT STATE?]
  Yes → Evidence-bounded conditional judgment
  No  → ASK minimum clarifying question (capability-specific, not label-based)
  ↓
[SOUL Explanation]
  → What the place requires
  → What friction exists
  → What alternatives exist
  → Traveler decides
```

## 8. Hyangiram Worked Example

**Scenario H-2:** "부모님 모시고 향일암 가도 괜찮을까요?"

### Traveler State:
- Companion: parents (age unstated)
- Currently in Yeosu area

### Label-based approach (inadequate):
→ "Hyangiram is difficult for elderly" → recommendation with no capability basis

### Condition-based approach (preferred):

**Experience Requirements (from HY-001/HY-002/HY-003):**
- Steep uphill approach from entrance
- Multi-segment steep stairs, ~400 steps (stone + iron)
- Narrow one-person stone gate passages (crouching required)
- Alternative flat/bypass road (right side) → reaches upper area but NOT stone gates
- Return: same route burden in reverse

**Traveler Condition needed:**
- Stair capability (key variable)
- Slope/incline tolerance
- Walking capability (baseline)

**Friction Assessment:**
- Stair capable: full temple access possible with pacing and rest
- Stair difficult: alternative route available → partial temple access (no stone gates)
- Neither route manageable: visit not advisable (Founder safety boundary)

**SOUL behavior (ACCEPT_PROVISIONAL_WITH_BOUNDARY):**
- ASK: "부모님이 가파른 계단을 오르내리는 게 괜찮으세요?"
- If YES: explain pacing + rest approach + full route
- If PARTIAL: explain alternative (우회로) + what it cannot access (돌문)
- If NO: QUALIFY → alternative route also involves incline; gently surface Founder safety boundary
- EP-3 KNOWN_LIMIT: descent-specific elder friction not directly verified; qualify descent as structurally symmetric to ascent route used

## 12. Safety Boundary (Research/Governance Wording — NOT Production Policy)

**SOUL may:**
- Explain known experience friction (from evidence)
- Ask about traveler-reported condition (capability, not diagnosis)
- Explain alternatives and their limitations
- Surface official operator eligibility restrictions
- Qualify uncertainty (EP-3 KNOWN_LIMIT)
- Suggest awareness of overexertion risk (Founder boundary)
- Leave final choice to the traveler

**SOUL must NOT:**
- Diagnose medical conditions
- Infer medical fitness from age or any other proxy
- Guarantee safety of an activity
- Decide physical capability from demographic label alone
- Tell a traveler to ignore official operator restrictions
- Convert traveler-reported fear or sensitivity into a medical diagnosis

**This wording is research/governance scope only.** Production policy requires canonical approval process.

## 14. Minimum ASK Implication

**Current approach risk:** Asking "부모님이 고령이세요?" or inferring difficulty from "부모님" → demographic label as decision variable.

**Condition-based minimum ask:**
"계단이나 경사진 길을 걷는 게 불편하신 분이 함께 가시나요?" (mobility capability clarification)

**Why this is better:**
- One question vs. potentially multiple age/health questions
- Directly targets the decision variable (stair capability)
- Elicits actionable information for friction assessment
- Respects traveler autonomy — they know their capability better than a demographic inference

**Analogous improvements (CONCEPTUAL):**
- Boat: instead of "어르신이세요?" → "배를 타실 때 멀미 경험이 있으세요?"
- Zipline: instead of inferring from age → "높은 곳이나 빠른 속도가 많이 부담스러우세요?"

**Note:** Exact wording is NOT frozen here. Only the information need is defined. Final wording belongs to Prepared Knowledge / Response Protocol design.

## 17. Known Limitations

1. **HY-003 EP-3 not directly verified:** The descent-specific elder friction (EP-3) remains PARTIAL_PASS. The model uses structural inference for descent burden, not a directly observed elder descent account.
2. **Boat/zipline examples are conceptual only:** No Yeosu boat or zipline evidence has been collected. These are hypothesis extensions, not supported facts.
3. **Capability elicitation is self-reported:** The model depends on travelers accurately reporting their capability. Over-confidence bias is a known risk.
4. **Official restrictions retain precedence:** Where operators have formal eligibility restrictions (health, weight, age limits), SOUL must surface these — they are not overridden by traveler self-assessment.
5. **Not all friction is physical:** The model focuses on physical capability. Traveler Condition also includes sensory, cognitive, or emotional dimensions — not addressed here.

## 18. What Is NOT Concluded

- This hypothesis does NOT mean "Hyangiram is unsuitable for seniors" — that would be the label-based error this hypothesis rejects.
- This hypothesis does NOT mean "all elderly travelers can visit Hyangiram" — that would ignore the real friction.
- This hypothesis does NOT prove the condition-based model is superior to any other approach in production.
- This hypothesis does NOT establish that competitor systems fail at this.
- This hypothesis does NOT authorize a new schema, ER, or architecture change.
